# Official device imagery

Downloaded on 2026-10-16 from the official CTR Electronics store and its Shopify CDN. `sources.json` records the exact source URLs and product pages. Downloaded bytes are unmodified; the CDN supplies the requested 384-pixel variants. Images are bundled for offline use.

Copyright and trademarks remain with CTR Electronics and the respective product/photography owners. These assets are not relicensed under Panther Tuner X's MIT source license. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` at the repository root.

The images represent device families. The Talon FX family uses an official Kraken X60 render; discovery does not distinguish every compatible motor or wiring variant. Unknown models use a neutral device icon rather than an incorrect product photo. The same loader supplies device cards and the Info panel.
